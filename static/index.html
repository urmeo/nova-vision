Why NovaVision

Features

Image prompts guided by emotion labels

Emotion Labels from Text

The classifier scores seven emotion labels from your words and uses the leading label to guide the image prompt's mood.

Palette and lighting cues

For non-neutral text, valence and arousal estimates add palette and lighting cues to the prompt alongside your selected visual style.
